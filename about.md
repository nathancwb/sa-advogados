# About SA Advogados

> **Strategic Legal Counsel in Brazil (Florianópolis/SC and Porto Velho/RO)**

---

## 🏛️ Institutional Overview
**SA Advogados** is a premier Brazilian law firm recognized for delivering high-stakes legal solutions combining academic rigor with practical commercial agility. Headquartered in **Florianópolis, Santa Catarina** with a full branch office in **Porto Velho, Rondônia**, the firm advises corporate groups, healthcare institutions, physicians, investors, and public entities throughout Brazil.
